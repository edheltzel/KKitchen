1. In a large bowl, whisk together the milk, white vinegar, salt and pepper. Add the chicken, cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, overnight if you can.

2. Combine the gluten-free flour, cornstarch, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, paprika, thyme, oregano and basil in a gallon-sized resealable bag and shake (or mix in a shallow dish).

3. Heat the oil in a 10" cast iron skillet (or a large skillet) over medium heat to 350°F. Test it by sprinkling in a little of the flour mixture: it should sizzle but not smoke.

4. One piece at a time, lift the chicken out of the marinade and put it in the bag. Seal and shake to coat, then set it on a baking sheet. Repeat with all the pieces.

5. Fry 3 or 4 pieces at a time, skin side down first, without overcrowding the pan. Oil temperature drops when the chicken goes in, so adjust the heat to keep it gently cooking. Fry each piece 15-18 minutes, turning with tongs about every 2 minutes, until golden brown and 165°F inside.

6. Move the chicken to a cooling rack or paper towel-lined baking sheet and let it rest at least 10 minutes. Serve warm or cold.
