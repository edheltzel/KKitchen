1. Preheat the oven to 375F/190C. Add the cottage cheese, sweetener and eggs to a large bowl and mix with a whisk or food processor. Add the salt, baking powder, cocoa powder and whey protein powder through a sifter or sieve into the bowl. Stir or whisk those ingredients in, followed by the walnuts and chocolate chips, which are both optional.

2. Line a small baking pan, like a 5x9-inch loaf pan, with parchment paper, or spray it with some cooking oil or use shortening. Add all the batter to the pan and bake in the 375F/190C oven for 20 to 25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

3. Allow it to cool and then cut into squares.
