# Real iPhone checklist

Things headless Chrome cannot prove. Check these on a real iPhone (Safari and the installed home-screen app).

- [ ] Tray open with the keyboard up: tapping Filters hides the keyboard, the sheet slides up, and Done is reachable without scrolling.
- [ ] Ratings survive close and reopen in the home-screen app: pick "I'm Ed", rate a recipe, swipe the app away, reopen, and the rating and the "I'm Ed" choice are still there.
- [ ] Rating buttons and the "I'm Ed / I'm Kristina" picker are easy to hit with a thumb (44pt feel), and the Undo bar sits clear of the keyboard-lifted search bar.
- [ ] Two-phone edit check (Ed rates on one phone, Kristina on the other, quick edits on both, nothing lost): only provable once the real shared store exists. The local stand-in keeps ratings on one phone only, so skip this until then.
- [ ] Separate storage on iPhone: Safari and the home-screen app keep separate storage, so pick "I'm Ed" (or "I'm Kristina") again after installing the app. Safari ratings won't appear in the app. With the shared store only the picker repeats, because the ratings live on the server.
- [ ] Week plan, swipe on a real phone: swipe left on a dinner. A half swipe rests open on a red Remove, a long swipe says "Release to remove" and removes when you lift your finger, and swiping up and down still scrolls the page. The outlined Remove button works too.
- [ ] Week plan, the Undo bar replaces the tab bar: after Remove, "Took X off Day" sits exactly where the tab bar was (same height, clear of the home indicator), the Week tab stays active when the tab bar returns, and it returns after Undo or 10 seconds.
- [ ] Week plan, planned dinners survive close and reopen: plan two dinners, swipe the home-screen app away, reopen, and both are still on their days. A removed dinner stays removed.
- [ ] Week plan, Safari and the home-screen app keep separate plan storage, like ratings: a dinner planned in Safari will not show in the installed app. Plan again after installing. With the shared store the plan lives on the server, so nothing repeats.
- [ ] Week plan, Plan button and day picker on a recipe card: the sheet is thumb-friendly (every day at least 44pt), a taken day says it replaces, a recipe on another day says it moves, and "Moved from Tuesday" shows afterwards.
- [ ] Week plan, a swipe must not also open the change sheet: swipe left on a dinner and let go; the change sheet stays closed (the click-swallow timer may behave differently in Safari than in Chrome).
- [ ] Last card clears the bars with the keyboard up and with the Undo bar showing: scroll to the very bottom of Recipes and of Week in both states; the last card (and on Week, Sunday and the hint) is fully visible, with nothing covering it.
- [ ] Recipe page, ticks survive closing the home-screen app: tick two ingredients, swipe the app away, reopen the same recipe, and both are still ticked. Reset checks clears them and "Checks cleared" offers Undo.
- [ ] Recipe page, Safari and the home-screen app keep separate tick storage: ingredients ticked in Safari will not show in the installed app. Tick again after installing. Ticks stay on each phone, with the shared store too.
- [ ] Recipe page, Back from a recipe returns to the same filters and scroll: filter and scroll the Recipes list, open a recipe, tap Back (and the Safari back swipe), and the list is where you left it.
- [ ] Recipe page, tick rows are easy to hit with a thumb (the whole row is the target, at least 44pt) and the Plan and rating buttons behave like they do on the list.
- [ ] Cards, tap just outside each button on a card (the Loved it, Not for us and Plan buttons, and the gaps between them) and see what opens: the recipe page should open, because the whole card is a tap target. Taps on the buttons themselves must not open it.
- [ ] Cooking mode, Wake Lock in Safari and in the installed home-screen app: start cooking, wait past your Auto-Lock time, and the "Screen stays on" pill is showing and the screen does not dim. If it does not work, the dashed "Screen may sleep" chip and the banner should show instead.
- [ ] Cooking mode, tap zones feel right with thumbs: a tap on the right side goes forward, the left side goes back, the Next step and Back buttons and the Use now rows keep their own taps, and selecting text does not turn the page.
- [ ] Cooking mode, the screen stays on for a 10 minute cook: follow a recipe slowly for 10 minutes without touching the phone between steps, and check the screen never locked (switch apps and back once too, it should ask for the lock again).
- [ ] Cooking mode, Exit and reload keep the place: on step 3, swipe the app away and reopen it (or reload), and you are on step 3. Exit goes to the recipe page in one tap with no question, and Back from there does not return to cooking.
